What changes, in short

  • Dark window on a deepslate texture (#16171A ground, #202226 cards), the portal's dark theme colours.
  • A pixel-art banner across the top with the logo, the name in Pixelify Sans (an open-licence pixel face) and the server's state as a pill. The banner is drawn for this server: spawn's forest, the cherry grove, the cave under it.
  • Tabs become a strip under the banner. Play is a bevelled green block, Vote is copper. Everything else keeps Segoe UI at 13 px, so nothing gets harder to read.
  • Player heads next to who's online, the pinned news item in the server card, "Since last time" as its own card.
  • The Minecraft font, textures and logo are Mojang's and are not used. Everything here is drawn fresh.
