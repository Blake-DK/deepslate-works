## 6. What worked, and what to keep doing

- Every script that changes a world ends on one PASS or FAIL, refuses when a condition is not met and keeps what
  it replaces. Three refusals today were all correct: a missing container, a stale log and an unapproved source.
- A set or a source counts only after Alex has read its numbers and said yes. The approval is recorded with its
  checksums and the scripts refuse anything else.
- Read the report before the next step. The one time the planner took "it was fine" in place of a log, the proof
  was never pulled.
- Ask what state a switch is in before a step that depends on it. "We're live" was assumed off for a day and it
  had been on since 2026-10-03.
- A merge to main does not rebuild the app unless the installer changed. Check before a deploy, because the
  installer tag would otherwise point at a build Alex has not tried.
